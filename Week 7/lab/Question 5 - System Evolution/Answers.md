# Question 5 - System Evolution

## Task

A growing startup adds cloud services, devices, and remote users but has no structured process for updating security controls. Identify the missing area of security administration.

## Answer: C

The missing element is **a procedure for adapting security as the system evolves**. Controls that fit an earlier environment can become incomplete or ineffective as new services and access paths are added.

In the lecture's four-area model, procedures for system evolution belong to **security planning**. Continual vigilance also appears in the security plan: someone must be responsible for maintaining protection as the system changes.

A practical response would be to:

1. Record new services, devices, data flows, and access requirements.
2. Review their security impact and the suitability of existing controls.
3. Assign an owner and deadline for necessary changes.
4. Update configurations and supporting documentation.
5. Review the results and revise the baseline periodically and after significant changes.

This is an illustrative process. The scenario establishes that the process is missing; it does not establish that every existing policy or risk assessment is absent.

## Why the other answers do not fit

| Option | Explanation |
|---|---|
| A | Policy definition establishes goals and responsibilities. The stated failure is the lack of a continuing process to adapt controls to growth. |
| B | The scenario concerns changing services and configurations, not primarily locks, buildings, power, or physical access. |
| D | Risk analysis can inform updates, but the most direct match is the missing system-evolution procedure explicitly described in the question. |

## Remember

**A security plan needs maintenance responsibility and review procedures as well as an initial rollout.**
