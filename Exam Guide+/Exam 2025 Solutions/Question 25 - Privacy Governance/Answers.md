# Question 25 - Privacy Governance

## Task

Classify board awareness of privacy risks, maintenance of a risk register, and assignment of data-protection accountability.

## Answer: B

These are organization-level governance activities. They establish oversight, responsibility, risk management, and decision-making arrangements for privacy, making **governance framework establishment** the best answer.

An inventory and an impact assessment can support governance, but neither alone describes the board-level oversight and assignment of accountability in the scenario. The question asks for a conceptual classification, not a conclusion that the company has fulfilled every GDPR obligation.

## Assessing the alternatives

- A: a data inventory records data and processing information, rather than constituting the whole oversight structure.
- C: penetration testing examines technical vulnerabilities and does not establish board responsibility.
- D: a privacy impact assessment evaluates the effects and risks of a processing activity; it is narrower than the described governance arrangement.

## Remember

Board oversight + assigned responsibility + maintained risk decisions point to governance.
