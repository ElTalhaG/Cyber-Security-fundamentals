# Question 5 - Non-Repudiation

## Task

Identify the main purpose of non-repudiation.

## Answer: B

Non-repudiation provides evidence linking an action to an entity so that the entity cannot credibly deny it without addressing that evidence. A verified digital signature can contribute evidence that a particular key signed particular content.

This is not a claim that cryptography alone proves human intent in every dispute. Attribution also depends on key custody, identity binding, records, and the surrounding process. For this conceptual question, B is the choice about evidence of who performed an action.

## Assessing the alternatives

- A concerns integrity.
- C concerns confidentiality.
- D concerns real-time detection.

## Remember

Non-repudiation is about attributable evidence of an action, rather than secrecy or general attack detection.
