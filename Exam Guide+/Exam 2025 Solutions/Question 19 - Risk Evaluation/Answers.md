# Question 19 - Risk Evaluation

## Task

A risk is assessed as high likelihood but low impact. Choose the next action within the described risk-management process.

## Answer: B

Compare the assessed risk with the organization's acceptance criteria and tolerance, then decide whether to accept, monitor, or require treatment. B is the only option that makes the response depend on those criteria.

Low impact does not mean zero risk. A frequent small loss can matter, and acceptance still needs an accountable decision and appropriate review. The scenario's own process also includes applicable policy and regulatory constraints; a company's preference alone cannot override those constraints.

No numerical likelihood, loss, or tolerance threshold is given, so it would be unjustified to claim that this risk definitely must be reduced or definitely must be accepted.

## Assessing the alternatives

- A ignores proportionality and the stated evaluation criteria.
- C treats low impact as permission to ignore the risk entirely.
- D is unnecessary solely because the evaluated risk is frequent but low impact.

## Remember

Risk evaluation compares the assessed risk with criteria; it does not automatically treat every frequent event the same way.
