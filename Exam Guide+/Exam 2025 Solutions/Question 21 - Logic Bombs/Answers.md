# Question 21 - Logic Bombs

## Task

A malicious payload activates after a particular financial transaction and deletes records and issues false reports. Identify the most likely malware type.

## Answer: B

A logic bomb is malicious code triggered by a defined condition or event. The transaction is the trigger, followed by the destructive payload.

Malware categories can overlap: a Trojan could carry a logic bomb. The question asks for the most distinctive explanation of the conditional activation, which is B. The paper labels this question “Links,” but the actual scenario asks about malware and a trigger.

## Assessing the alternatives

- A: a Trojan is characterized by deceptive appearance or purpose, not specifically by this event-triggered behavior.
- C: a rootkit is associated with concealed privileged persistence or hiding malicious activity.
- D: a worm is characterized by self-propagation; no replication is described.

## Remember

Identify the defining behavior: conditional trigger → logic bomb; propagation → worm; concealed privileged presence → rootkit.
