# Question 20 - Operational Security Planning

## Task

An endpoint detection tool produces excessive alerts without tuning, escalation rules, or thresholds. Identify the failed planning principle.

## Answer: B

The missing element is an operational process and maintenance plan for making the control usable over time. Analysts need alert prioritization, escalation criteria, ownership, tuning, and a feedback process to distinguish actionable signals from noise.

Installing a tool is not enough if the organization cannot operate it effectively. Alert volume can overwhelm staff and lead to important alerts being dismissed. The scenario directly describes absent procedures, rather than absent cryptography or access restrictions.

## Assessing the alternatives

- A: multiple independent defenses may help overall security, but layering alone does not fix an unusable alert workflow.
- C: least privilege concerns restricting access; it is not the missing alert-handling plan.
- D: key rotation does not address the stated tuning and escalation failure.

## Remember

A control needs an owner, operating procedures, useful thresholds, and lifecycle maintenance.
