# Question 6 - Arbitrated Protocols

## Task

Two parties trust a third party to monitor and validate their normal message exchanges. Choose the protocol type.

## Answer: B

An **arbitrated protocol** uses a trusted third party during ordinary execution. The subsidiaries rely on that party to help ensure the exchange proceeds correctly.

An adjudicated protocol normally proceeds without that intervention and calls on a trusted adjudicator if a dispute arises. The question describes ongoing monitoring and validation, so the active-arbitrator model fits.

## Assessing the alternatives

- A: a self-enforcing protocol does not rely on the described active trusted arbitrator.
- C: adjudication is normally a dispute-resolution role rather than participation in each normal exchange.
- D: peer-to-peer describes an interaction structure and does not capture this trusted-third-party role.

## Remember

Arbitrator during the normal exchange; adjudicator when a dispute needs resolution.
