# Question 24 - Vishing

## Task

An impersonated bank representative calls and asks for account details. Classify the attack.

## Answer: B

Vishing is phishing performed through a voice interaction, such as a telephone call. The attacker borrows the bank's authority and a security-verification pretext to persuade the victim to disclose information.

Phishing is sometimes used as a broad umbrella for social-engineering messages, but vishing is the most specific choice given the channel in this question.

## Assessing the alternatives

- A is less specific than the offered voice-specific term.
- C, smishing, uses SMS/text messaging rather than a voice call.
- D, tailgating, is following an authorized person into a restricted physical area.

## Remember

Voice → vishing. SMS → smishing. Physical follow-through of a door → tailgating.
