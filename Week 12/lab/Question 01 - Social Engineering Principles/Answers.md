# Question 1 - Authority and urgency

## Scenario / question

Maya is pressured by a caller claiming to be a regional director. The caller speaks commandingly and imposes a five-minute deadline.

## Answer

**A** - Authority and urgency are the strongest drivers of the password disclosure, though the wording also includes familiarity and consensus cues.

## Reasoning

The claimed director role and commanding tone invoke **authority**. The outage and five-minute deadline create **urgency**. Those cues directly pressure Maya to disclose the password. However, the caller also says he has worked with her team before (**familiarity**) and that everyone responds quickly (**consensus**). Since those two cues map directly to option C, the question is ambiguous about which pair it considers “primary.” **A** is the best answer if the focus is on the authority and deadline driving the disclosure; **C** is defensible if the quoted familiarity and group-norm claims are the intended cues.

## Takeaway

Authority and urgency is identified by the mechanism and evidence in the scenario. Read the details carefully: channel, target, impersonated identity, and the actual effect often distinguish similar terms.
