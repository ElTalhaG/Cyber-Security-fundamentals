# Question 6 - Vishing plus email spoofing/phishing

## Scenario / question

An attacker phones HR pretending to be the CEO’s assistant, then follows with an email forged to look like it came from the CEO.

## Answer

**B** - Vishing plus email spoofing/phishing.

## Reasoning

The phone call is **vishing**, a voice-based form of phishing. The follow-up email uses **email spoofing** and phishing to reinforce the false request. The two channels are coordinated to make the same impersonation seem credible.

## Takeaway

Vishing plus email spoofing/phishing is identified by the mechanism and evidence in the scenario. Read the details carefully: channel, target, impersonated identity, and the actual effect often distinguish similar terms.
