# Question 3 - Phishing

## Scenario / question

The employee receives a deceptive email that pretends to be IT and asks them to click a password-reset link.

## Answer

**C** - Phishing.

## Reasoning

This is phishing: a message impersonates a trusted organization and tries to make the recipient disclose information or visit a fake site. Vishing uses voice; smishing uses SMS; whaling targets senior executives specifically.

## Takeaway

Phishing is identified by the mechanism and evidence in the scenario. Read the details carefully: channel, target, impersonated identity, and the actual effect often distinguish similar terms.
