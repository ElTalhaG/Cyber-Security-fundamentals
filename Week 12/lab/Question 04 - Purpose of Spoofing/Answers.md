# Question 4 - Impersonating a trusted entity

## Scenario / question

The question asks for the primary purpose of spoofing in social engineering.

## Answer

**D** - Impersonating a trusted entity.

## Reasoning

Spoofing falsifies an identity or source so the target believes a message, caller, or site is associated with a trusted entity. A false pretext may be part of the attack, but impersonation is the best general answer. Voice deception is vishing, and redirecting web traffic is a possible technical effect rather than the definition.

## Takeaway

Impersonating a trusted entity is identified by the mechanism and evidence in the scenario. Read the details carefully: channel, target, impersonated identity, and the actual effect often distinguish similar terms.
