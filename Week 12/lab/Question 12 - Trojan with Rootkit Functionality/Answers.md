# Question 12 - Privileged Trojan with rootkit functionality

## Scenario / question

A fake hardware-utility installer places a kernel driver that hides malware files, processes, and registry keys.

## Answer

**B** - Privileged Trojan with rootkit functionality.

## Reasoning

The installer masquerades as legitimate software, which is Trojan behavior. The kernel-level component hides the malware and its artifacts, which is rootkit functionality. The combined choice B captures both the delivery disguise and concealment behavior.

## Takeaway

Privileged Trojan with rootkit functionality is identified by the mechanism and evidence in the scenario. Read the details carefully: channel, target, impersonated identity, and the actual effect often distinguish similar terms.
