# Question 7 - Whaling plus domain spoofing

## Scenario / question

A tailored message to the CFO uses confidential business details and an almost identical domain to request an urgent transfer.

## Answer

**B** - Whaling plus domain spoofing.

## Reasoning

The CFO is a senior executive, and the message is highly tailored to that person, so this is **whaling** (targeted spear phishing against an executive). The one-character lookalike domain is **domain spoofing**. A standard bulk phishing or SMS attack does not fit.

## Takeaway

Whaling plus domain spoofing is identified by the mechanism and evidence in the scenario. Read the details carefully: channel, target, impersonated identity, and the actual effect often distinguish similar terms.
