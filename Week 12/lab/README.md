## Wording notes

Question 1 clearly contains **authority** and **urgency**, but it also explicitly cues **familiarity** and **consensus**. A is best if “primarily” means the pressures driving disclosure; C is defensible if the quoted relationship and group-norm claims are the target cues. The individual answer explains the ambiguity.

Question 11 describes ransomware, which the lecture distinguishes from crypto-mining malware. Since “ransomware” is not offered, **B** is the closest/intended option, but the terminology is imprecise.
