# Question 8 - Whaling using a synthetically generated voice

## Scenario / question

The CEO’s voice requests a wire transfer, but location logs conflict with the call and the audio contains synthetic-sounding artifacts.

## Answer

**A** - Whaling using a synthetically generated voice.

## Reasoning

A custom voice imitation of the CEO is a **synthetic-voice/deepfake** attack. Because the target and requested action are a CFO-level financial transaction impersonating the CEO, the social-engineering campaign is also whaling. Traditional vishing describes the voice channel but misses the synthetic-voice clue.

## Takeaway

Whaling using a synthetically generated voice is identified by the mechanism and evidence in the scenario. Read the details carefully: channel, target, impersonated identity, and the actual effect often distinguish similar terms.
