# Question 5 - Carding

## Scenario / question

The question asks which listed activity is not social engineering: dumpster diving, shoulder surfing, carding, or spear phishing.

## Answer

**C** - Carding.

## Reasoning

**Carding** is fraudulent use or trading of payment-card details. Dumpster diving and shoulder surfing gather information by exploiting human or physical weaknesses; spear phishing deceives a specific target. Carding is a payment-card fraud activity, not itself a social-engineering technique.

## Takeaway

Carding is identified by the mechanism and evidence in the scenario. Read the details carefully: channel, target, impersonated identity, and the actual effect often distinguish similar terms.
