# Question 11 - Crypto malware (closest listed option); technically ransomware

## Scenario / question

The company’s documents become unreadable and a popup demands cryptocurrency for restoration; no theft is reported.

## Answer

**B*** - Crypto malware (closest listed option); technically ransomware.

## Reasoning

The behavior described is **ransomware**: malware encrypts or otherwise locks files and demands ransom. Option B is the only plausible intended choice, but the lecture deck distinguishes ransomware (slide PDF p. 54) from **crypto-malware**, which it defines as malware that mines cryptocurrency using the victim’s resources (p. 64). Since ransomware is absent from the choices, B is the closest available option, but the wording is technically imprecise.

## Takeaway

Crypto malware (closest listed option); technically ransomware is identified by the mechanism and evidence in the scenario. Read the details carefully: channel, target, impersonated identity, and the actual effect often distinguish similar terms.
